### `experiment02_xrd_crystallinity/` — PdO on Al₂O₃, XRD crystallinity of the Batch-1 Sobol films (delivered 2026-08-27)

- **Input:** `raw_data_received/pdo/08_27_26/PdO_data_0827.xlsx` — θ-2θ patterns for the 14 valid Batch-1 conditions × 3 replicates (`sobol` sheet, IDs `replicate_run`), the #14 recipe at 9/11/13 nm on HfO₂/Si (`scaling thickness`), and the old 5 nm recipe (`old_recipe`); Hall data in `benchmark`.
- **Metric:** relative PdO(101) crystallinity index from `utils/xrd_crystallinity.py` — unclipped 33–35° area above a linear baseline (the campaign's AUC objective), as % of the mean of the three largest detected areas (`ref_area = 333.93`; `--ref best_run` uses the highest recipe mean instead, 305.63 = #9); `crystallinity_pct_thk` divides by final thickness first (`ref = 23.88 / nm`). Constrained Gaussian fit gives detection (SNR ≥ 3), FWHM and apparent Scherrer size. The classic crystalline/(crystalline+amorphous) ratio is **not** computable on these thin films (the sapphire-subtracted halo is a scan artefact, identical for amorphous and crystalline films).
- **Result:** 19/42 films show a resolved PdO(101) peak. Every 25 °C film ≤ 12 nm final and every 425 °C film ≤ 4 nm is amorphous (index 11–26 %, no peak); 425 °C films ≥ 13 nm show a clear peak (68–92 %), #7 (25 °C, 14 nm) 58 %, #21 (425 °C, 9 nm) 47 % — exactly the fab team's slide claims. Replicate SEM² is exported for the GP. Films without a peak still read ~10–25 % (weak nanocrystalline signal + noise floor): treat as "below detection", not a fraction.
- **Crystallinity vs Hall:** condition-level Spearman ρ = −0.70 with log carrier concentration, +0.52 with mobility (`xrd_crystallinity_vs_hall_0827.xlsx`, `crystallinity_vs_hall_0827.png`); #15 is the low-crystallinity / high-mobility exception.
- **Outputs-only correlation and ANOVA (§7, added 2026-09-24):** Spearman correlation among the three outputs (film level, n = 41 / condition level, n = 14): crystallinity vs mobility +0.47 / +0.55, crystallinity vs log₁₀ carrier −0.67 / −0.72, mobility vs log₁₀ carrier −0.57 / −0.61 (all p < 0.05). Type II ANOVA of an additive linear model per output on the 41 films: crystallinity is driven by Dep Thickness (partial η² 0.72), Ox Temp (0.23), Dep Temp (0.18); mobility by Ox Temp (0.78), Ox Press (0.77), Dep Thickness (0.57), Dep Press (0.49); log₁₀ carrier by Ox Temp (0.83), Dep Thickness (0.71), Vac Ann Temp (0.31). R² 0.88 / 0.95 / 0.93 but the lack-of-fit test is significant for all three (replicate noise ≪ what the additive model misses): a first-order screen, curvature / interactions are left to the GP.
- **XRD reference card (received 2026-09-10, ICDD PDF 00-041-1107, PdO):** a database fingerprint (line positions, hkl, relative intensities with (101) = 100), not a measured scan — it confirms the peak assignment and shows the analysed peak is the unresolved (101)+(002) pair, but carries no counts, thickness or instrument scale, so it **cannot** be the 100 % reference of the index. `python utils/pdf_card_to_readable.py` writes a readable export into this folder (`pdf_card_00-041-1107_PdO.xlsx` / `.md`, the card's own stick chart, and `pdf_card_vs_measured_film_0827.png`, card vs film 03_14).
- **Files:** the folder is empty by default (cleaned 2026-09-24) — the notebook writes nothing unless `SAVE_OUTPUTS = True` in its setup cell; `OUT_FILES` there names each item and any entry set to `None` is skipped: the design-space table (§1), the raw-pattern overview (§2), the amorphous-halo diagnostic (§3), the metric demo and the per-film fit QA (§4), the by-condition / thickness-normalised / vs-thickness figures and their presentation variants (§5), the HfO₂ despike demo (§6), crystallinity-vs-Hall, pair plot, factor effects, outputs correlation and ANOVA figures + tables (§7), and the CLI tables `xrd_crystallinity_<cli_tag>[_hfo2_thickness|_old_recipe].xlsx` (per_sample / per_run / references / config) plus `xrd_crystallinity_vs_hall_0827.xlsx` (§8).
- Notebook (study of record, all steps and the why-not-the-classic-ratio diagnostic): `analysis/pdo/experiment02_xrd_crystallinity_0827.ipynb`

### `sampler_bo_1d_test/` — Sobol+BO vs LHS+SA+BO end-to-end check

- 1 continuous + 1 two-level categorical toy problem, identical GP (`utils/mobo.fit_gp`) + LogEI, 30 seeds; only the initializer differs. Result: statistical tie with a slight LHS+SA edge — no reason to prefer Sobol. Notebook: `doe/pdo/sampler_bo_1d_test.ipynb`; shared code: `utils/doe_bo_test.py`.
- Also holds the `hd_*` files from the 3-problem, 6-arm follow-up (`doe/pdo/sampler_bo_hd_batches_test.ipynb`; Hartmann-6 smooth / Shekel-4 deceptive / Michalewicz-5 rugged; pure BO, ± space-filling batches, and small-init + qNEI batches; fixed 60-eval budget, arms precomputed by parallel workers via `utils/run_doe_bo_arm.py`). Results: pure BO wins or ties on average; space-filling batches cut tail risk where BO locks in; batch-mode qNEI loses little vs sequential BO — but with a small init the initializer matters (LHS+SA(8)+qNEI ≫ Sobol(8)+qNEI on Hartmann); on deceptive/rugged problems total budget binds. Batch-policy isolation (§4, matched 12-pt init, all-batch delivery): qNEI batches ≫ SF-only batches everywhere (SF-only is the worst arm — quantifies the value of BO); a mixed 6 qNEI + 2 SF batch costs little and never wins — acceptable insurance, not a performance play.
